# MathBuddy — specification and asset-first planning

A proposed native iPad math app for children ages four to eight, initially centered on a five-year-old: beautiful pictures, hands-on math, meaningful animations and rewarding creative play.

**Current stage: Phase 1 creative-direction review checkpoint.** Two pending art directions, a motion study and provisional contracts are available in the [sample gallery](assets/production/picnic-v1/review/index.html). Natural voice generation is blocked by unavailable credentials; ten exact audition requests are preserved. Native implementation and bulk pilot asset production remain future separately authorized phases.

## Start with the review checkpoint

[Independent review and planning checkpoint](docs/review-checkpoint.md) brings together the findings, their disposition, the exact proposed asset/content catalogs, the detailed native phase plan and the decisions still to make.

## Product documents

1. **[Feature specification](docs/feature-spec.md)** — features, learning progression, screen flow, concrete activities, rewards and native iPad requirements.
2. **[Asset plan](docs/asset-plan.md)** — character/world, math pieces, states, motion, voice scripts, production batches and review criteria.
3. **[Phased roadmap](docs/roadmap.md)** — product agreement → creative direction → pilot asset pack → separately planned native phases → family review → expansion.

Pip’s Picnic is the recommended first world. The theme, art, voice, starting skill band and reward format remain review choices. The process takes useful lessons from [Pebble](docs/research/brightpebble-lessons.md), with the user's requested asset-first sequence.

## Existing experiments

An experimental native pilot, browser studies and draft assets were produced prematurely. They remain intact as **unapproved experiments** and are not the approved product baseline. No roadmap phase is marked complete because those files exist. See the [experiment register](docs/experiments/README.md).
