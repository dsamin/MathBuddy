Editable provisional wordmark

Unmasked icon · small-size inspection

The 1024 square export is opaque. Rabbit silhouette and scarf persist at small sizes; facial detail becomes limited at 16 pixels. Physical home-screen visibility and platform masking remain pending.

Exact opaque 1024 icon · Preserved generated original

Wordmark source, font license and reproducible recipe · Branding inspection and provenance

Sources are six separate generated raster artworks. The exported dimensions use disclosed resampling and registration. OpenRaster lets each registered raster layer be painted, masked or replaced independently; it is not vector or editable-object artwork.

Catalog slots stay region-relative and reproject by identity. No 80–88 pt touch, larger-text, actual object-fit or physical-device usability claim follows from these canvases. Final selected delivery remains a separate decision.
